WAINUIOMATA CAMP - 2 DAYS

Meeting Information

Time: 8:30am

Location: Front of school hall - look for your form class

Form classes will be grouped together around the perimeter of the car park. You need to have your day pack/backpack with lunch, water bottle, sun hat, warm jumper/thermal and rain jacket. Plus one overnight bag with gear listed below. Please wear appropriate sports clothing with lace up sports shoes with good grip - no denim.

Want to see where your kids will be staying? Click here.

Day 1 Activities

Once the roll has been taken we will catch a private bus to Camp Wainui where you will first construct a tent with your friends and put your overnight bag inside. We have a large camping area at the back of the camp as well as use of the hall, kitchen and toilets in the huts.

Raft Building

Learn how to build rafts which also includes how to fit lifejackets correctly and how to use throw ropes for water safety.

Emergency Bivvy Making

Develop essential survival skills by learning how to construct emergency shelters using natural materials and basic equipment.

Search and Rescue

Practice search and rescue skills and work together in team challenges to solve problems in outdoor scenarios.

There will be relaxing time before dinner in the hall. Dinner will be chilli mince and pasta (veg option available) with grated cheese and bread rolls, plus tinned fruit and ice cream for dessert. In the evening we will play camp games before lights out at around 9:30pm.

Day 2 - Orongorongo Track

Distance: 5.2km each way

Location: 7kms from camp - Catchpool carpark in Remutaka Valley Park

We will wake nice and early and have breakfast of cereals, toast and spreads. Tents will be packed down and overnight bags packed, then left in a pile ready for buses to pick them up in the afternoon.

You will take your day pack with your second lunch, water bottle, rain jacket and warm top/hat and board the bus to Catchpool carpark. From here you will walk the Orongorongo track in and out of the valley. On the way you will learn some map reading skills and about local flora and fauna.

Learning Activities in the Valley:

  • Learn how to build your own hobo stoves for cooking in the bush
  • If the river is at its usual height of up to calf depth and not fast flowing, we will learn about river crossings (cancelled if river is deeper or faster)
  • Have lunch in the valley surrounded by nature

Then we will head back along the track to the car park where a private bus will pick us up and transport us back to school for around 3:30pm.

Camp Rules

No alcohol, vaping, smoking, or drugs. Follow all rules at all times. Once it's lights out, all students must stay in their own tents. Breaking these rules will result in parents being called to pick up student with possible further consequences.

Food & Water

Bring your own packed lunches for both days. Please ensure you have enough food to sustain you on the 10km walk and for activities at camp. Water bottles can be refilled from camp taps.

Devices

Not many powerpoints available for charging phones. Bring a power bank or use aeroplane mode to preserve battery. You should not be on devices whilst away - turn phone back on when you arrive back at school.

Showers

A few showers are available but we strongly discourage their use where possible as it takes a while for 260 people to shower and we are only away for one night.

RANGITUHI WALK

A private bus will drop students at the start of the Rangituhi walkway. Teachers will help students navigate the path to the lookout and then up to the summit of Rangituhi. From here, students will traverse the tops and then pop down into Te Ngahere o Tawa.

Students will spend more time on the walk learning about reading maps, trees and leaves, local places of interest from viewpoints etc.
