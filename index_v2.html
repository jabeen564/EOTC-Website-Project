As part of the Year 10 programme, students will be participating in four days of Education Outside the Classroom (EOTC). The focus of this programme is one of citizenship and social awareness.

FOUR DAYS OF ACTIVITIES

Students will participate in the following activities throughout the week

COMMUNITY SERVICE

Give back to the local community

RANGITUHI WALK

Experience the outdoors

WAINUIOMATA CAMP

Team building adventures

Trip Schedule

Each student will be given a timetable informing them of which trip they will do on which day. These trips will run from Tuesday 3 December to Friday 6 December. All of these trips will depart from Tawa College at 8:35am and will return by 3:20pm. Click on the links to see your Timetable for the Week: Group 1, Group 2, Group 3, Group 4.

Cost and Payment

The total cost of the trips is $75.00 per student. This includes transport and the cost of all activities. Due to the nature of the activities and the fixed costs involved, there will be no refunds given. Payment is due before Friday 25 October. Please submit payment to the student office.

What to Bring

Students are expected to bring a packed lunch on all of these days, as they will not have any opportunities to purchase lunch. Students will be required to wear appropriate mufti clothing on all days. This involves good walking/sports shoes, raincoat, sun block, and suitably warm clothing.

Student Expectations

On all trips, students will be expected to abide by the school rules. This includes absolutely no cigarettes, drugs or alcohol. Students will be expected to conduct themselves in a manner that is respectful to both the supervising teachers and other students. It is essential that all students play their part in risk management and follow staff instructions at all times.

Permission Forms

If you have not already returned the permission forms given out earlier this term, then please collect another copy from the school office, and hand them in to the student office along with $75.00 before Friday 25 October. If you have already returned the permission forms, then you only need to pay the $75.00 before Friday 25 October.

Complete Your Registration

Don't forget to complete your online registration form! This form collects important information about dietary requirements, medical conditions, and emergency contacts. Please fill out the form as soon as possible to ensure we have all the necessary details for a safe and enjoyable EOTC week. Note: Please log in with your school account to access the form. Click here to register.

Important Information

All these activities are compulsory but have been designed to be both educational and fun. Students will be supervised at all times by Tawa College staff. Students will not be allowed to participate in these trips if they do not have the correct gear. If you have any difficulty supplying these required items, please indicate on the return form and we will endeavour to assist where possible.
