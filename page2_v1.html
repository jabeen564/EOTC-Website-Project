ABOUT COMMUNITY SERVICE

Students will complete one day of community service assisting local residents and businesses. This will usually occur within walking distance of Tawa College for all Year 10 students. This day has the key focus of citizenship and has been a great success in previous years.

Students will spend a day completing community service in a small group with friends in the Tawa basin, supervised by a staff member. This is a highly valuable experience where students have the chance to interact with the elderly helping them with gardening, painting etc or assisting local food banks, kindergartens, hospice shops and charities for the day.

COMMUNITY IN ACTION

DAY TIMETABLE

9:15 am – 9:45 am

Meet in the school gym for roll call and briefing. Staff will outline safety guidelines, distribute job allocations, and confirm supervision groups before heading out.

9:45 am – 10:00 am

Students walk with their assigned groups and supervising teacher to their community service locations within the Tawa area.

10:00 am – 12:30 pm

Begin morning community service activities — gardening, painting, assisting at kindergartens, food banks, or local charities. Students will work in small teams to support local residents and businesses.

12:30 pm – 1:00 pm

Lunch break. Students can rest, eat, and rehydrate at their work sites or a nearby safe area under teacher supervision.

1:00 pm – 3:00 pm

Continue afternoon community service tasks, completing any remaining jobs and tidying up work areas. Staff will check progress and assist where needed.

3:00 pm – 3:15 pm

Groups return to Tawa College, sign out with their supervising teacher, and debrief briefly about their experiences before dismissal.

COMMUNITY SERVICE ACTIVITIES

Students will participate in the following activities throughout the day

GARDENING & PAINTING

Students will help local residents by tidying gardens, planting flowers, and painting fences or walls. This hands-on work supports the community and brightens shared spaces around Tawa.

FOOD BANKS

Students will assist local food banks by sorting, packing, and distributing food to families in need. This activity helps provide essential support to those facing hardship in the community.

KINDERGARTENS

Students will spend the day helping at local kindergartens — supporting teachers, engaging with children, and assisting with creative or outdoor activities.

HOSPICE SHOPS & CHARITIES

Students will lend a hand in local hospice shops and charities by organising items, helping customers, or assisting with store displays. Their support helps raise funds for important community causes.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

+ What do I need to bring?

A day pack with your lunch, water bottle, sun hat, warm jumper, rain jacket, and any medication you need. Wear old clothes suitable for gardening, painting, or charity work, and closed-toed shoes.

+ Can I work with my friends?

Yes! Students will usually be in small groups with friends, supervised by a staff member.

+ Will we be walking far?

Most jobs are within walking distance of Tawa College. A very small number may involve a short drive with staff supervision.

+ What if it rains?

The day will still go ahead unless there is torrential rain. Bring a rain jacket and warm clothes just in case.
